Calendar 2026

Personal

I am always fascinated by the concept of a calendar, whether it represents time that has passed and the memories, or times to come and all the dreams of the future. It is time materialized into a graphically appealing sheet of paper, or a few, depending on the style of the calendar.

I often design a calendar for the coming year as a personal practice, and this is the one I did for 2026. Weekends are always what I look forward to the most, so I decided to reflect that in my calendar. The weekends get the most vibrant colors of the gradient for the excitement, and the rest of the week builds up to it.
